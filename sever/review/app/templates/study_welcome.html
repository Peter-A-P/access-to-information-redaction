Thank you for taking part

You are about to review {{ study.tasks|length }} short records the way an access-to-information analyst would: deciding, for each piece of personal information, whether it is released to the requester or withheld. Each record is one page. It takes most people about an hour, and you can stop and come back at any time.

Every person and record here is invented. Nothing you see is real.

How it works

  1. The records come in two halves. In one half the tool has already proposed what to withhold and why, and you accept or overrule each proposal. In the other half there are no proposals and you find everything yourself. Each half starts with a practice page that is not counted.
  2. With proposals: every highlighted span has three buttons. Withhold accepts the proposal, Release leaves the text in the record, and Escalate withholds it and marks it for a second opinion. If the tool missed something, select the text with your mouse and press Add severance.
  3. Without proposals: select each piece of text that must be withheld, check the category, and press Add severance. If you make a mistake, press Undo beside it.
  4. When the page is done, press Mark page complete, then Next record.

Two things that matter

  • Work at your normal pace. Each page is timed from when it opens until you mark it complete. Accuracy matters as much as speed: being fast and wrong does not help the study.
  • Take breaks between records, after you press Mark page complete and before you press Next record. The clock is stopped there.
